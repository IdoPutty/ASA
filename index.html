About

Shaping Space,
Shaping Experience.

Adi Shmueli is a Tel Aviv–based architect with over a decade of practice rooted in the conviction that buildings are not merely functional objects, but carriers of cultural memory, human scale, and the quality of light. Every project begins with a single question: what does this place want to become?

Her studio ASA operates across residential, cultural, and commercial typologies — from intimate apartments in New York and Jerusalem to civic cultural centers and landscape structures in northern Europe. Each project is developed with a small, focused team, maintaining a close relationship with clients throughout design and construction.

Adi holds a Master of Architecture with distinction from the Technion — Israel Institute of Technology, and completed postgraduate research at the Architectural Association in London. She is a licensed member of the Association of Engineers and Architects in Israel and has lectured at the Bezalel Academy of Arts and Design.
